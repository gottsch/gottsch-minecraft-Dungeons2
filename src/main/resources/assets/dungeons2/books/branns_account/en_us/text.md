title: Brann's Account
author: Brann Holt, who was not pleased

![](item:minecraft:spyglass)

Oswin writes everything down. Oswin also walked us into a floor that was not there. So here is my account, and it is shorter, and it is right.

There are two things that rule a hole this deep. I have seen both. I am alive. Listen.

---

**The eye.** It floats. It looks at you, and its looking hurts. The orbs it throws pass straight through a shield as if it were paper. Fire and stone you can block. The rest you cannot, not with any shield a smith ever made.

It has a beam, too, and the beam goes through walls. I watched it burn a hole in good stone to get at me. Do not trust a wall.

---

I said no shield a smith ever made. A **mirror** is another matter. Whatever the eye throws, a polished face throws back. Oswin went looking for one. I hope he found it.

---

**The bull.** Horns, a fist the size of my head, and one idea: walk at you and hit you. It does not stop for walls. If it is stuck, it breaks its way out, and it does not take long about it.

You cannot outrun it in these halls, so do not try. Keep your feet planted when it hits, if you can. Being thrown is how it gets the second blow.

---

That is all I know. I am going back up the stair, which is more sense than Oswin has.
