title: Oswin's Margins
author: Oswin Marl, delver

![](item:minecraft:map)

Found a map in a barrel on the first floor. Brann says a map of a hole is a waste of a good barrel. Brann can find his own way, then.

It was blank when I picked it up. I carried it, and it drew what I saw: every room I walked into, every passage I lit. The stair down is marked in red. The stair up is marked in gold. Remember which is which. I nearly didn't.

---

The trouble is, it only knows the one floor. I took it down the stair and it was a map of the floor above me, and no use at all.

So here is the trick, and I am proud of it. Lay the map out beside a clean **empty map**, and put a stick of **coal** with them, or charcoal, it does not mind which. Work them together and you get the map back, and a second one besides, smudged and blank.

---

Carry the blank down to the next floor and use it there. It takes the shape of whatever floor is under your boots. It works in other holes too, not only this one. I have tried.

Leave out the coal and you only get a copy of the map you already had. I did that twice before I understood.

---

Brann swears there are compasses that point the way to holes like this. He has seen exactly one, and it was in a dead man's chest at the very bottom.

I'll keep my maps.
