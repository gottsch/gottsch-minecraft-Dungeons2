---
title: The Wall That Wasn't
author: Brann Holt, who was right
---

![](item:minecraft:lever)

Oswin thinks he is the clever one. Oswin walked past this six times. I found it.

Some passages end in nothing: a dead end, a blank wall, and a torch or two to light it. The builders here did not waste stone on nothing. When a passage ends for no reason, there is a reason.

---

**Count the torches.** Two sconces beside a bare wall, where one would light it well enough. One of them is not a sconce. Take hold of it and pull. The wall is a door, made of the wall's own stone, and it opens.

---

Behind it is a room the dungeon kept for itself. There is a chest, and it is only a chest. I checked, after the barrel. And there is a stand in the middle with one thing on it, set out like a gift.

Oswin's mirror was on a stand like that. So it may be what you came down for.

---

**Before you take it, look at the floor.** Sometimes the stand is only a stand. Sometimes the floor all around it is held up by nothing but the thing on top. Lift the prize, and that floor goes, in a ring, all the way out, and there are spikes at the bottom.

The stones by the walls hold. Know which way you mean to jump before you reach for it. You get one breath.

---

I jumped the right way. Write that down, Oswin.
