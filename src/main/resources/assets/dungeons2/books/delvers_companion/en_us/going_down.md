A dungeon has one to five floors, each a maze of rooms and corridors. Stairs and shafts lead down to the next. Every floor is harder than the one above it, and its loot is better.

The way in doesn't always land you in the middle. You might arrive in a corner, with the whole floor stretching away in one direction.

At the very bottom waits the dungeon's master. See [Bosses](entry:bosses).
