```spotlight dungeons2:mirror_shield
Raise it to turn eye-rays and gazes back on whoever cast them.
```

An ordinary shield stops fire and stone, but the eye-magic of the floating watchers passes straight through it. This one doesn't let it.

Keep it raised: lowered, it can still be knocked out of your hands.
