A dungeon has its own inhabitants, and they change as you go down: skeletons of many kinds, the restless dead, oozes, animated armour, gargoyles, orc warbands and rats.

```entity dungeons2:skeleton_warrior
A common guard of the upper floors.
```

They spawn throughout the rooms and corridors, and some rooms keep producing more until you deal with them. Ordinary zombies, skeletons and spiders turn up too, but creepers never spawn inside a dungeon.

Light keeps the spawning down. See [Bring Light](entry:bring_light).
