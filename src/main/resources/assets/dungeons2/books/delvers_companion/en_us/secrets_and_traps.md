## Hidden doors {#doors}

Some dead ends aren't. A secret room hides behind a wall, with a way to open it close by. Count the torches, and look closely at any that seem out of place.

What's kept in a secret room is usually worth the search.

## Floors {#floors}

Some floors aren't as solid as they look, and some prizes are safer left where they are.

The stones by the walls hold.
