```spotlight dungeons2:mining_chest
One to a dungeon.
```

The builders dug a long way through the rock to make these halls, and kept some of what they dug out. Each dungeon has a single Mining Chest, holding a share of the ore from the stone around it.

It's never as good as the master's chest, but it's worth finding.
