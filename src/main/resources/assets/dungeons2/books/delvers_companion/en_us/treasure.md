---

- **Chests** get richer the deeper you go.
- **Weapon racks** mostly hold what the dungeon's guards left behind. Now and then one holds a weapon worth taking.
- **Pots** break, and sometimes something falls out.
- **Sealed tombs** might hold grave goods, or might hold their occupant. Open them ready for either.
- The **master's chest**, at the very bottom, is the best of all.
