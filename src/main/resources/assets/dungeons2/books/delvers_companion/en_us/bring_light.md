These halls have been dark a long time. Monsters spawn freely in the dark, and the darkness closes in the deeper you go.

The braziers you'll find are cold. Light them, or place your own torches, to make a room safe.

Some parts of a dungeon have flooded, and some run with lava. Watch where you step.
