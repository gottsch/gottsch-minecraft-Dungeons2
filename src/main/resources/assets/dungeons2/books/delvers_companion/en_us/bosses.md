Every dungeon ends in a master and its escort. Bigger dungeons hold bigger masters, and better rewards.

Once you're in, there's no tunnelling out. While the master lives, the stone of its room won't break, and explosions and pistons won't move it. You can't dig in under the chest, either.

Some masters are a lot easier with the right gear, and that gear is hidden on the way down.
