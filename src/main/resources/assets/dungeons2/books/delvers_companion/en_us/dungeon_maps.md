A Dungeon Map turns up in chests, barrels and pots, named for its dungeon and the floor it was found on. Carry it and it draws what you see: every room you walk into, every passage in your sight.

The stair **down** is marked in {dark_red}red{}. The stair **up** is marked in {gold}gold{}.

## A map for every floor {#copying}

A map only knows its own floor. To map the next one, copy it.

```spotlight dungeons2:blank_dungeon_map
Craft a Dungeon Map with an **Empty Map** and a **coal** (or charcoal). You keep the map, and get a Blank Dungeon Map besides.

Use the blank on any dungeon floor, in this dungeon or the next, and it becomes a map of that floor.
```

Leave out the coal and you only get an ordinary copy of the map you already had.
