---
title: The Delver's Companion
---

Somewhere under your world, someone dug down a very long way, built something, and left. This book is for going down after them.

It fills in as you go. Pages about things you haven't found yet stay closed until you find them, so nothing here gives away more than you've earned.

Carry it, and hover over anything you bring up from below: press the lookup key to open the page about it.
