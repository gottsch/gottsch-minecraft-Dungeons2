author: Oswin Marl, delver

![](item:minecraft:lantern)

I went down. Brann went up. One of us was right, and I am still not sure which.

There are things at the very bottom that are not lords of anything. They are older than the stair. I saw two of them, in two different holes, and I am writing them down while I still can.

---

**The tyrant.** An eye, like the ones Brann swore at, but dead, and larger, and worse. Its gaze locks your limbs where you stand, and while you stand there it brings help.

The mirror works. I found one. Hold it up and what it throws comes home to it. Do not lower it to look.

---

**The statue.** I walked past it. It was a carving at the end of a hall, and then I was in its hall, and it was not a carving.

It is slow. It does not need to be fast. It lifts its arms and brings them down, and the floor itself strikes you, and you are thrown. Watch the arms. When they rise, get out of the circle.

---

Arrows chip it. Swords barely mark it. It is made of stone, and there is one tool made for stone. I wish I had brought a better pick.

There are boots, they say, that drink the shock of a blow through the floor. If you find them, wear them.

---

My lamp is out. I can hear it coming down the hall.

If you found this, you are deeper than I meant anyone to come. Go back up.

Or bring a pick.
