![](item:minecraft:lantern)

Third day below. Brann says the stair goes down five floors. Brann has said many things.

We found the rats first, then the rats found us. Brann lost a boot to one the size of a dog. I did not laugh. I wrote it down instead, which he says is worse.

---

Note for whoever reads this: **watch the floor.** It looks like the rest of the floor. That is the point of it. If a stone shivers under your boot, do not stop to wonder why. You have a breath, perhaps two. Get off it.

Brann stopped to wonder. We found him, after. He was not pleased.

---

The barrels on the third floor. I counted them going in. I counted them coming out. There was one fewer, and it had teeth.

---

The tombs are sealed, and some of them are sealed for a reason. Lift a lid and you may get gold. You may get the one who owned the gold. You do not get both.

---

Something lives at the bottom. We have not seen it. We have heard it look at us, if that makes sense, and it does not. Brann says an eye that big must hate its own reflection.

Brann is wrong about most things. I am going to find a mirror anyway.

---

Last lamp oil. If you found this, the stair is behind you. Go back up it.

I am going down.
