author: Hollis Venn, sexton

![](item:minecraft:iron_shovel)

I kept the graves above this place for thirty years. I know what goes into the ground. I am learning what comes up out of it.

Whatever rules the bottom of this hole is no great lord. It is one of the lesser dead, or a chief of the living, and it keeps a court of smaller things around it. Know which one you face before you reach its door.

---

**The grey one.** Ash for skin, and eyes you will want to look into. Do not. The longer you hold its gaze the harder it strikes, and it strikes from across the room. Keep your eyes on its feet. The old diggers wore a carved gourd over the face for this, and I laughed at them once.

---

**The cold one.** It bites, and the bite takes more than blood. Each wound it gives you shrinks you, and it grows on what it takes. It calls the dead up to fight beside it and bends the living to its will. Do not trade blows with it for long. End it, or leave.

---

**The bone captain.** A skeleton that remembers how to lead. It stands at the edge of its pack with a shield up and makes the others stronger and faster. Kill the pack around it and it has nothing left to stand behind.

---

**The warlord.** An orc chief, broad as a door, with his warband about him. He rallies them as the captain does. Thin the band before you close on him.

---

Thirty years. I have buried better delvers than you. Read this twice.
